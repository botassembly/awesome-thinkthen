## Decentralized On-Chain AI Oracles (EVM & Smart Contracts)

Decentralized applications (dApps) in DeFi, autonomous governance, and

multi-signature attestations, introducing centralized points of failure

off-chain inferences via SNARKs , it incurs prohibitive off-chain
proving latencies (10–300 seconds) and high verification costs
(250,000–500,000 gas), rendering it fundamentally incapable of
